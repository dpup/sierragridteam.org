When a storm closes a pass or a power shutoff darkens the corridor, the question in most foothill households is a plain one: is the road open? Three official channels answer it for the Highway 4 and Highway 49 corridors, and each is worth setting up now, while the lights are still on.

Two of them are maps. [Caltrans QuickMap](https://quickmap.dot.ca.gov/) shows real-time chain controls, lane closures, and incidents on every state highway. The [CHP Traffic Incident Information Page](https://cad.chp.ca.gov/traffic.aspx) lists traffic incidents and emergency closures, searchable by area. Both are the first thing to reach for — and both need a working data connection.

That is the catch on a fire day or a public-safety power shutoff: the data connection is exactly what thins out first. Caltrans runs a voice line for the same information — [1-800-427-7623](https://roads.dot.ca.gov/roadscell.php), the Highway Information Network — that reads back road and chain-control conditions over an ordinary phone call. A call can complete on a copper landline, or on a single bar of voice signal, after a map app has given up. It is the channel worth writing down: program it into a phone and tape it inside a cabinet door.

Much of what these channels report through winter is chain control. Caltrans posts [three levels](https://dot.ca.gov/travel/winter-driving-tips/chain-controls) where the highways climb — the grade on Highway 4 above Arnold, Highway 108 on the Sonora side:

- **R-1:** "Chains are required on all vehicles except passenger vehicles and light-duty trucks under 6,000 pounds gross weight and equipped with snow tires on at least two drive wheels."
- **R-2:** "Chains or traction devices are required on all vehicles except four-wheel/all-wheel drive vehicles with snow-tread tires on all four wheels."
- **R-3:** "Chains or traction devices are required on all vehicles, no exceptions."

Higher up, two routes stop being a question each winter. Highway 4 over [Ebbetts Pass](https://goldrushcam.com/sierrasuntimes/index.php/news/local-news/74066-california-department-of-transportation-caltrans-announces-official-winter-seasonal-closure-of-state-route-4-ebbetts-pass-as-of-11-00-a-m-today-friday) and Highway 108 over [Sonora Pass](https://mymotherlode.com/news/local/10340278/mountain-passes-close-for-winter-season.html) gate shut for the season once the snow arrives. In its most recent closure, Ebbetts was gated eastbound at Lake Alpine and westbound at the State Route 4/89 junction. These stretches are not plowed through winter — the gate is the plan — and neither reopens until crews can clear it, usually ahead of Memorial Day weekend.

None of this takes long to set up, and it does not keep until the storm. Bookmark the two maps, and write the voice line somewhere that does not depend on a charged phone or a live tower. The map tells you the most on an ordinary day; the voice line is the one still standing when the map won't load.
